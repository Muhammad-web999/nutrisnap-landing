NutriSnap AI

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

NutriSnap AI ("NutriSnap", "we", "us") is a meal-scanning and nutrition-tracking app that works at nutrisnap.tech and as an Android app. We run it from Karachi, Pakistan. This policy explains what information we collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. You can reach us any time at support@nutrisnap.tech.

In short
  • We collect what is needed to run the app: your account, your profile, your food logs, and (if you choose) Google Health steps and weight.
  • Meal photos and chat messages are sent to Google's Gemini AI to produce your results. We do not keep your meal photos.
  • We do not sell your personal information, and the app currently shows no ads and has no advertising trackers.
  • You can delete your account and data yourself at any time inside the app (Profile → Delete My Account).

1. Information we collect

Account information

Your email address and display name. If you sign up with email, your password is handled by Google's Firebase Authentication — we never see or store your password. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email from Google.

Profile information you enter

Age, gender, weight, height, goal (lose, maintain or gain weight), activity level, daily calorie goal, and the username you choose. We use these to calculate your targets and to personalise AI advice.

Food logs and progress

The meals you log (name, calories, protein, carbs, fat, meal type, time, and whether it came from an AI scan), plus your points, level, streak, badges, and the challenges and rewards you claim. We also save the short weekly and monthly AI summaries written for Pro members.

Meal photos

When you scan a meal, the photo is sent to our server and on to Google's Gemini AI to identify the dish and estimate nutrition. We do not save the photo after the result is returned. Only the nutrition result you choose to log is saved.

AI nutritionist chat

When you chat with Dr. NutriSnap, your messages — together with your basic profile, today's calorie total and your most recent meal names — are sent to Gemini to write a reply. We do not store your chat history on our servers; it stays in your browser only while the page is open.

Google Health (optional, Pro)

If you connect Google Health, we ask for read-only access to your steps and weight. We store your latest steps and weight reading, and we may update your profile weight from it. The access tokens Google gives us are stored encrypted. You can disconnect at any time in Profile → Google Health; disconnecting deletes the connection and tells Google to revoke our access.

NutriSnap's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use Google Health data only to show you your steps and weight and to keep your profile up to date. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone else.

Payments

Subscriptions are processed by Creem, which acts as the "merchant of record" and collects your payment details and taxes. We never receive or store your card number. We receive your email, plan, subscription status, and renewal date so we can switch Pro on or off.

Friends and leaderboards

Your username, points, level and streak can be seen by your friends and, if you are in the top ten, on the public leaderboard. Friend requests show only usernames. We never show your email or real name to other users. Choose a username that does not reveal who you are if you prefer.

Feedback and emails

If you send us feedback, we save it. We send you a welcome email, payment emails, and — unless you unsubscribe — a daily reminder on days you have not logged a meal, a weekly report, and streak milestone emails. Every reminder and report email has an unsubscribe link. Essential emails such as payment receipts continue.

Technical information

Our hosting providers record technical data such as IP address, device and browser type, and request times in server logs, which we use for security and troubleshooting. Your browser stores your login session and a few settings (for example, whether you dismissed the install banner). The app's offline helper saves only an offline page and the app icon, not your data.

2. How we use information

  • To run the app: scan meals, keep your logs, calculate targets, and show your progress.
  • To personalise advice, such as your weekly and monthly summaries.
  • To run points, badges, friends and leaderboards.
  • To process payments and give Pro access.
  • To send the emails described above.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse, fix problems, and meet legal duties.

We do not sell your personal information. NutriSnap currently shows no ads and uses no advertising trackers. If that ever changes, we will update this policy first.

3. AI features and health disclaimer

Our AI features are powered by Google's Gemini API. Google processes the photos and text we send in order to return a result. Under Google's terms for its free (unpaid) service tier, Google's human reviewers may read and process content sent to the service to improve Google's products, after Google disconnects it from any account identifiers. Please do not put sensitive personal information (such as phone numbers, addresses, or medical details) into photos or chat messages.

NutriSnap gives general nutrition information and estimates. AI results can be wrong. NutriSnap is not medical advice and does not diagnose or treat any condition. If you have a medical condition, are pregnant, or have a history of disordered eating, please talk to a qualified professional before changing your diet.

5. Where your information is processed

Our providers process data in the United States and other countries, which may have different privacy laws from Pakistan or your country. By using NutriSnap you understand this.

6. How long we keep information

We keep your information while your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete your profile, meals, points, badges, claims, friends, feedback, Google Health connection and sign-in account. Payment records are kept by Creem as required for tax and legal reasons. Copies may remain for a limited time in routine backups and provider logs before they are overwritten.

7. Your choices and rights

  • See and edit your profile and username in the Profile tab.
  • Unsubscribe from reminder and weekly emails with the link in any of them.
  • Disconnect Google Health in Profile → Google Health.
  • Cancel a paid subscription from Profile → Manage Subscription on our website, or contact us.
  • Delete your account in Profile → Delete My Account, or follow the steps on our account deletion page.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion by emailing support@nutrisnap.tech from your account email. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights, such as to object to or restrict processing. We will reply within 30 days.

8. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), store Google Health tokens encrypted, limit who can access our systems, and check our code for security problems. No service is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password and tell us right away if you suspect a problem.

9. Age requirement

NutriSnap is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as NutriSnap grows. If we make an important change, we will update the date above and tell you in the app or by email.
