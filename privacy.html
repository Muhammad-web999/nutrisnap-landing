Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

NutriSnap AI ("we", "our", "the app") is a nutrition tracking application. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and how it's handled.

What we collect

  • Account info: your email address, name, and password (handled securely by Firebase Authentication — we never see or store your raw password).
  • Profile info you provide: age, gender, weight, height, activity level, and goals, used to personalize calorie/macro targets and BMI calculations.
  • Meal data: food names, calories, macros, and photos you choose to scan, used to track your nutrition and power app features like streaks, badges, and analytics.
  • Health data you connect (optional): if you link Google Health, we access step count and weight data you explicitly authorize, only to display it back to you and sync your weight.
  • Usage data: basic app activity (points, streaks, login times) needed for the app's core features to function.

How we use your data

  • To provide core app functionality: meal logging, AI meal analysis, progress tracking, gamification, and social features you opt into.
  • To send you emails you'd reasonably expect: welcome emails, streak celebrations, optional reminders, and weekly reports. You are not sent marketing spam.
  • Meal photos are sent to Google's Gemini AI to identify food and estimate nutrition — photos are processed for this purpose and are not used to train AI models by us.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your personal data to third parties.
  • We don't share your health or meal data with advertisers.
  • Friends you add via Social Features only see your name, points, and streak — never your meal history or personal profile details.

Third-party services we use

  • Firebase (Google): authentication and account security.
  • Railway & PostgreSQL: secure hosting of your account and meal data.
  • Google Gemini API: AI-powered meal photo analysis.
  • SendGrid: transactional and reminder emails.
  • Google Health API (optional): only if you choose to connect it.

Your rights

You can update or delete your profile information at any time from the Profile tab. To request full account deletion, contact us at the email below — we will remove your account and associated data within a reasonable timeframe.

Data security

We use industry-standard practices including encrypted connections (HTTPS), secure authentication via Firebase, and access-controlled databases. No system is 100% immune to risk, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated date.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach out at support@nutrisnap.ai.
