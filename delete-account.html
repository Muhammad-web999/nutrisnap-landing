Delete your NutriSnap AI account

You can delete your NutriSnap AI account and its data at any time. This page explains how, what is deleted, and what we may keep.

Option 1: Delete it yourself in the app (fastest)

  1. Open NutriSnap AI (the Android app, the installed web app, or nutrisnap.tech) and log in.
  2. Open the Profile tab and scroll to the bottom.
  3. Tap Delete My Account, type DELETE to confirm, and tap OK.

Your account is deleted straight away. If you have an active paid subscription, we cancel it first so you are not charged again. If we cannot cancel it automatically, the deletion stops and the app tells you what to do.

Option 2: Ask us to delete it

If you cannot log in, email support@nutrisnap.tech from the email address of your account with the subject Delete my account. We may ask you to confirm it is you. We will delete your account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What we delete

  • Your profile and username.
  • All your logged meals, points, level, streak, badges, and claimed challenges and rewards.
  • Your friends and friend requests.
  • Your Google Health connection and saved tokens (and we ask Google to revoke our access).
  • Your feedback messages and saved AI summaries.
  • Your sign-in account (email and password).

What we may keep

  • Payment records. Our payment provider Creem keeps invoices and transaction records as required for tax and legal reasons. We do not store your card details.
  • Backups and logs. Copies may stay in routine backups and provider logs for a limited time before they are overwritten.

Want to delete only some data?

To disconnect Google Health only, open Profile → Google Health → Disconnect. To stop reminder and report emails, use the unsubscribe link in any of them. For anything else, email us at support@nutrisnap.tech.

Questions about your data? Read our Privacy Policy or email support@nutrisnap.tech.
